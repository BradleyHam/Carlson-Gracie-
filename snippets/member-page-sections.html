The journey

There are no shortcuts here

The average black belt takes about ten years. That is the point. Every belt is earned on the mat and tied on by your professor, and each one still means what it meant in Carlson's day.

WhiteDay one
BlueAround 2 yrs
PurpleAround 5 yrs
BrownAround 8 yrs
BlackAround 10 yrs

Typical timelines, not promises. Promotion is at your professor's discretion.

Your first class

Walking in is the hardest part

Wear

Shorts and a t-shirt are fine. We have clean gis to borrow, so there is nothing to buy before you know you love it.

Expect

A warm-up, one or two techniques, and light drilling with a partner. You roll when you are ready, not before.

Bring

Water, a towel, and no ego. Every black belt in the room was once the nervous person by the door.
