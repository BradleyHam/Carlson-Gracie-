Seminars & events

World-class jiu-jitsu, on our mats

Some of the best grapplers on the planet have taught on our mats, right here in Queenstown. When a big name tours New Zealand, the call tends to come to Jose, because people know he takes this seriously and looks after his guests.

Open to everyone

We don’t lock the doors

Every seminar is open to all our members. These athletes teach, they roll with the room, and half the time they’re out to dinner with the crew afterwards. Rolling with someone you’ve only ever watched compete, in your own town, is a rare thing. It comes with your membership.

Tournaments and competing

None of it feels strange

We’ve run comps to full IBJJF standard around the area for ten years. So when our members step onto a mat in Sydney, Tokyo or Vegas, they’ve seen the set-up and they know the drill. Want to compete? There’s a whole crew who travel and back each other. Don’t want to? No pressure at all.

02CornerHelp a teammate through every round.
03SupportVolunteer, learn to referee, or just be there.
04TrainNever compete at all. The choice is yours.
