Gear & apparel

APE, made by grapplers

APE is our own fightwear, designed and founded by Jose so members could get gear that fits, lasts, and doesn’t cost you a fortune in disappointment.

About Our Method Our Story Why Carlson Gracie Coaches Gear Seminars with Jose

The name

Why APE?

Because apes were the first grapplers. Long before any of us, they were rolling around on the ground sorting things out. We liked that. The design is clean and simple, no clutter, and that’s the way Jose wanted it.

Tried and trusted

We train in it. We compete in it.

So it’s tested to bits before it ever reaches you. People go hunting for a cheap gi online, it turns up baggy and rough, and they end up back at the academy trying ours on. Everyone who does is surprised by how good it feels.
