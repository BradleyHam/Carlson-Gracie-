Seminars with Jose

Bring Jose to your gym

Jose travels to teach. Thirty years of real Carlson Gracie jiu-jitsu, a warm room, and a seminar your members will still be talking about weeks later. If you run an academy and want him on your mats, here’s how it works.

What he brings

Traditional, efficient jiu-jitsu. Taught the way it was passed to him. Strong foundations for the newer members, and sharper, specific games for your competitors. Gi or no-gi. He reads the room and pitches it to the level in front of him, so everyone walks away with something they can use on Monday.

Why Jose

Known and trusted across New Zealand

A real Carlson Gracie lineage, three decades on the mat, and the coach who brought jiu-jitsu to Central Otago and built a family of academies from scratch. He’s brought world-class names into the country himself, and he’s as good with a room of white belts as he is with a room of black belts.
