Adults All Levels · Gi & No-Gi    /training/adults
The main adult program.
Adults, all levels
Gi and no-gi, every level
This is where most of the academy lives. White belts and black belts on the same mat, learning the same thing, pushing each other along. It’s friendly and it’s competitive at the same time, and that mix is the whole magic of the place.
2 · HOW A CLASS RUNS
We teach in blocks, a few weeks on one position, so you go deep instead of skimming the surface. Every class is a warm-up, some technique, drilling, then rolling. You don’t learn jiu-jitsu by watching it. You learn it by doing it, over and over, with someone who knows more than you.
3 · GI OR NO-GI?
The gi is grips, control and patience. No-gi is faster and sweatier, more scrambles. Most people end up doing both and loving them for different reasons. Try one, try the other, and see which one grabs you.
4 · NEW TO IT
If you’ve never trained, don’t overthink it. Most people find their feet in the beginners and fundamentals classes first, then step straight into this room. Either way you’ll be looked after. They’ll guide you, give you tips, and go easy while you find your feet. The only person who can make jiu-jitsu dangerous is you, and we won’t let you do that.

The structured entry pathway. A dedicated place to learn the basics before the all-levels classes.
Your first weeks on the mat
Beginners and fundamentals
This is the front door. A dedicated class to learn the basics properly, at a pace that suits someone brand new, before you step into the all-levels sessions. No experience, no pressure, and nobody expecting you to know a thing.
2 · HOW IT WORKS
We teach the fundamentals in blocks, a few weeks on one foundation at a time. Posture, escapes, the handful of core positions everything else is built on. You’ll drill them until they feel natural, and you’ll roll gently and safely while it all sinks in. Get these right and the rest of jiu-jitsu opens up.
3 · WHO IT’S FOR
Total beginners, and anyone coming back after a break who wants to sharpen the basics. You don’t need to be fit, flexible or tough. You just need to turn up and be willing to learn.
4 · WHERE IT LEADS
Once the foundations are there, you’re ready for the all-levels adults classes, and Fabio will make sure you’re set. Most people are surprised how quickly they go from lost to holding their own.

Our Method    /about/our-method
The differentiator page. Jose never wrote his method down, so this puts it on paper: tradition, foundations, culture.
How we teach
People think all jiu-jitsu is the same. Walk into three gyms and you’ll find out fast that it isn’t. Every academy teaches differently, whether they’ve ever said so out loud or not. Here’s ours, and it comes down to three things.
2 · TRADITION
We’re Carlson Gracie, right down to the warm-up. We run the mats, we line up, and we show respect to each other and to the room. We’re not precious about it. You won’t get told off for the wrong colour gi. But that old-school respect is what turns a room full of strangers into a proper academy.
3 · STRONG FOUNDATIONS, TAUGHT IN BLOCKS
We don’t chase whatever’s trendy this year. We build deep foundations and teach in blocks, one idea at a time over a few weeks, so it actually lands. And we roll every single session. You can watch a hundred videos, but you get good by doing.
4 · CULTURE THAT CARRIES YOU
This is the part nobody can copy. Welcoming and competitive at the same time. The higher belts pull the newer ones up. Everybody has a hand in somebody else’s journey. It’s why people stay for years, and why the ones who move away still call this place home.
“I keep the traditional jiu-jitsu alive, and I’m open-minded enough to bring in the new things that make people better.” JOSE GOMES

Our Story    /about/our-story
The New Zealand journey. The pioneer years, the growth.
It started with one blue belt and no plan
Jose Gomes found jiu-jitsu at 16, back in Brazil. He’d tried karate and capoeira first, and neither one grabbed him. Jiu-jitsu did. What hooked him was that he could roll for real from day one, instead of tapping pads and pretending.
2 · A LONG WAY FROM HOME
New Zealand wasn't much of a decision. A mate mentioned an island at the bottom of the world you could get into on a three-month visa, and that was enough. Jose landed with barely a word of English and no idea what came next, a gym least of all. He just wanted to see somewhere new, and to keep training while he was there.
3 · TEACHING FROM SCRATCH
There was no jiu-jitsu in Queenstown. None in Central Otago. So Jose started teaching, for one simple reason. He wanted people to train with. He earned his purple, brown and black belts here, flying home for camps and leaning on the odd visiting black belt while he had no coach of his own. Slowly, teaching became coaching. Coaching became a school. The school became a family.
“I never counted on it, so I was never afraid it wouldn’t work. It’s something I do because I love it.” JOSE GOMES
4 · FIFTEEN TO TWO HUNDRED
Fifteen people became more than two hundred. One mat in Queenstown became a family of academies across the South Island. Jose never chased any of it, which is exactly why it worked.

Why Carlson Gracie    /about/why-carlson-gracie
The heritage and lineage argument.
Why Carlson Gracie
Carlson Gracie is one of the biggest names in the history of the sport, and it’s not a name we wear lightly.
2 · WHAT CARLSON DID
Carlson threw the doors open. While parts of the Gracie family wanted to keep jiu-jitsu close, he spread it to everyone he could reach. That’s the spirit we’ve kept. To us the name means tradition, hard work, and a door held open for anyone who walks up to it.
“There are gyms all over that come from Carlson Gracie. They just changed the name. We stayed real.” JOSE GOMES
3 · WE KEEP IT REAL
Plenty of academies started under Carlson Gracie and quietly rebranded to chase their own thing. We didn’t. Look back far enough and a huge slice of the jiu-jitsu world traces home to Carlson. We’re proud to still carry the name, and to carry it properly.
4 · OUR LINEAGE
Ours runs from Carlson Gracie through [Alexandre Penão] in Curitiba, to [Rodrigo Zanon], to [Roger Ulet], Jose’s professor to this day. Real jiu-jitsu, passed hand to hand, all the way to our mats in Queenstown.
“I don’t think we’re better than anyone. But there’s something in our culture that’s special. It’s unique. No one else is going to get it.” JOSE GOMES

Start Here    /membership/start-here

Never done jiu-jitsu? Start here.
If you’re nervous, good. Everyone was. Every black belt in this room was once the person hovering by the door, wondering if they’d made a mistake. Here’s everything you need, so you can walk in knowing exactly what happens next.
2 · WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENS IN A CLASS
A warm-up to get moving. One or two techniques, taught slow. Some drilling with a partner. Then rolling, if and when you’re ready, never before. From the door it can look like a fight. It isn’t. It’s a game of leverage and timing, closer to chess than boxing.
“The only person who makes it dangerous for you is yourself. And in our gym, you don’t get to do that, because we won’t let you.” JOSE GOMES
3 · WHAT TO WEAR AND BRING
Shorts and a t-shirt for your first go. Water and a towel.
4 · YOUR FIRST MONTH
Most people walk in a bit timid and, three months later, they’re part of the furniture. Chatting before class. Hanging around after with questions. Getting a big grin from the room when they come up the stairs. That shift, from stranger to family, is the whole point of this place.

FAQ · FOOTER
Frequently asked questions    /faq
Answers the real signup fears Jose named, cancellation and price first. Add more from the CMS over time.
CAN I CANCEL ANY TIME?
Yes, and we mean it. No lock-in. Train for a couple of weeks, and if it’s not for you, one phone call sorts it. We’d rather you felt free to give it a proper go.
HOW MUCH IS MEMBERSHIP?
[$XX a week] for adults, [$XX] for kids, with casual and visitor rates on the membership page. We put the prices up front so there are no awkward surprises.
I’VE NEVER DONE JIU-JITSU. WHERE DO I START?
Right here. Head to Start Here, or just book a free class. Our beginner and fundamentals classes are built for people on day one.
DO I NEED TO BE FIT TO START?
Not at all. You train at your own pace from day one, and the fitness comes from the training itself.
WHAT SHOULD I BRING TO MY FIRST CLASS?
Shorts, a t-shirt, water and a towel. We’ll lend you a clean gi, so there’s nothing to buy before you know you love it.
ISN’T IT JUST FIGHTING? WILL I GET HURT?
It looks like a fight from the outside, but it’s a game of leverage and timing. Leave your ego at the door, come to learn, and it’s safe. In our gym, the higher belts make sure of it.
WHAT AGES ARE THE KIDS’ CLASSES?
Ages 5 to 12. Games first, technique always, and no striking, ever.
GI OR NO-GI, WHICH SHOULD I PICK?
Try both. The gi is about control and patience, no-gi is faster and more scramble-heavy. Most people end up doing both.
I’M ONLY VISITING. CAN I DROP IN?
Always welcome. See the visitor rates and the timetable, and come as you are.
HOW MUCH ARE PRIVATE LESSONS?
From [$110] an hour, depending on the coach.
WHAT MAKES CARLSON GRACIE DIFFERENT?
A real Carlson Gracie lineage, a deep roster of black belts, world-class seminars in our own town, and a family of academies across the South Island. Most of all, a culture where everyone has a hand in helping everyone else get better.
