Never done jiu-jitsu? Start here.

If you’re nervous, good. Everyone was. Every black belt in this room was once the person hovering by the door, wondering if they’d made a mistake. Here’s everything you need, so you can walk in knowing exactly what happens next.

What actually happens in a class

A warm-up to get moving. One or two techniques, taught slow. Some drilling with a partner. Then rolling, if and when you’re ready, never before. From the door it can look like a fight. It isn’t. It’s a game of leverage and timing,closer to chess than boxing.

In Jose’s words

“The only person who makes it dangerous for you is yourself. And in our gym, you don’t get to do that, because we won’t let you.”

Jose GomesHead professor · 3rd degree black belt

What to wear and bring

Walking in is the hardest part

Wear

Shorts and a t-shirt for your first go. We’ll lend you a clean gi, so there’s nothing to buy before you know you love it.

Bring

Water and a towel. That’s the lot.
